# Supplementary Evidence: Decision-Specific Uncertainty Comparisons

These earlier experiments retain their frozen populations, actions, comparison families and inference rules. They provide context for decision-specific uncertainty evidence and are separate from the main B/G and executable-utility questions. Moving their presentation does not change any estimate or statistical role. Full machine rows remain in T7_previous_seed_UQ.csv and TABLES.json.

The frozen 1,536-query full-pipeline comparison estimates a positive multi-seed UQ increment of +0.003952, simultaneous interval [+0.001330,+0.006985]. It supports the increment for that pipeline and population.

The 1,024-query fixed-action risk-head comparison estimates full-common at -0.00007971, envelope [-0.00361242,+0.00345300], meeting its prespecified ±0.005 rule. The full-support comparison remains unresolved because its event guard is not met. The latter cannot be called equivalent on the basis of an interval crossing zero.

These experiments differ in populations, upstream actions and estimands. Comparing their outcomes does not test whether placing uncertainty at one decision stage rather than another changes its incremental value. Such a stage interaction would require a matched design and a direct interaction contrast. The later result neither retracts the earlier positive result nor establishes general efficacy or ineffectiveness of UQ.

Native ENTR uses entropy of a single predictive distribution. The earlier multi-seed statistics have separate definitions and cannot be substituted for that score. For current matched fixed-action generation-confidence comparisons, see T3 and RQ2; those comparisons retain their own supervision and adopted-count contracts.
