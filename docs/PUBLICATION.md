[English submission release](https://github.com/chak6229/RAG-rene-icpc2027/releases/tag/rene-review-20261125-english)

The release contains the English review edition of the study cited as version 2026.11.10 in the manuscript. It includes the replication ZIP with supplementary tables S1–S25, the seven frozen experiment data archives and their index, and the three study model archives W1, W3, and W5. The manuscript PDF is supplied separately.

Use an authorized account to access the private repository. The [model manifest](assets/STUDY_ASSETS.json) records the download URLs and SHA-256 hashes. See the [repository README](../README.md) for replay and installation instructions.
